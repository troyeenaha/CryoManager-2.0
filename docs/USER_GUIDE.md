# CryoManager: quick guide for lab members

**Getting access.** Open the CryoManager link and click **Request an account**. Confirm your email.
A lab administrator will then approve you. The page updates on its own once they do.

**Find a sample.** Go to **Reports** and type anything into *Find a sample*: name, cell line, sample ID
(S-000123), position (B7), box, researcher or passage number. Click a result to jump straight to its spot in the grid, highlighted in amber.

**Add a sample.** **Grid View** → choose the box → click an empty (dashed) square → fill in the
form → **Save**. The position is filled in for you. Only *Sample Name* is required. Open
*Advanced Details* for vial count, project, markers and notes.

**Several vials in one slot?** Set *Number of Vials* in Advanced Details. The square shows ×3 and
so on. A slot never holds two separate records.

**Move a sample.** Click the sample → **Move** → pick the destination box and an empty square →
confirm. The move is recorded in the sample's **History** tab.

**Take a sample out.** Click it → **Remove / Archive** → choose *Archive* (used, thawed) or
*Removed* (discarded) and add a short reason. The slot becomes free, and the record stays searchable
(Reports → Filters → Status).

**Someone else changed it first?** If two people edit the same sample at once, the second save is
stopped with a message. Close the form; it reloads with the latest data. Nothing is overwritten silently.

**Roles.** *Lab Members* manage samples. *Read-Only* users can look, search and download reports.
*Administrators* also manage tanks, racks, boxes and users.
